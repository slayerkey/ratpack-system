# PC Power Meter Lite Art Review

## Result

**PASS**

The five marketplace frames are visually distinct and follow the canonical XENEON sequence: cover, feature/value breakdown, broader product showcase, settings/interaction, and size compatibility.

The hero has clear hierarchy, strong XENEON device dominance, readable title/subtitle, and the PackRat rat logo only in the footer center.

The measured sensor scope is readable and does not imply universal whole-PC power. Energy math, no-gap behavior, session metrics, and sensor selection are explained without overcrowding the frames.

The settings/gallery frame uses distinct Wide Meter, Vertical, Compact, and Measurement Details states with safe label spacing.

The size frame clearly shows S, M, L, and XL compositions and communicates eight tuned layouts across horizontal and vertical orientations.

No cover/gallery image is duplicated. No footer wordmark is present. No text collision, clipping, obvious glyph damage, or misleading power claim was found in the reviewed contact sheet.
