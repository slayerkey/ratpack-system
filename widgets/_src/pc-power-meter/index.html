What this meter measures

Metrics are calculated only from the selected iCUE power sensor.

Session energy integrates real power readings over the actual elapsed time between valid samples. Missing or disconnected intervals are not filled in.

PC Power Meter never adds CPU and GPU power together and calls it total PC draw. Whole-system wording is used only when the selected iCUE sensor itself identifies that scope.
