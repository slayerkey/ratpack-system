# Peripheral Battery Panel Rat Art review

Status: `APPROVED`

Version: `1.0.0`

Price: `$6.99`

The deterministic Rat Art candidate is approved for the release candidate.

## Visual review

The hero keeps the actual widget as the dominant visual and preserves readable product positioning above it.

Battery urgency reads immediately through the sorted percentages and fill bars. Charging remains visually distinct from charge level through the persistent badge and patterned treatment.

The feature frame explains the product without overcrowding the actual widget capture.

The settings frame shows full board, vertical, compact, and touch detail states using deterministic captures of the real widget.

The sizes frame communicates the different XENEON compositions without shrinking the product into unreadable decoration.

The standard thin fragmented detail along the top XENEON device bezel belongs to the approved shared device plate, not the widget capture, and is accepted as nonblocking.

## Provenance

All customer facing widget imagery comes from deterministic Playwright captures of Peripheral Battery Panel with fictional local battery sensor fixtures.

The fixtures include multiple charge levels, charging and discharging states, duplicate device names, an explicit provider time value, a nonbattery sensor, and a disconnected battery sensor.

The marketplace compositor uses the approved calibrated XENEON Edge device plate and canonical repository Rat Art tooling.

Image generation is disabled. No ImageGen, DALL E, image API, or other generative image provider is part of the production art path.

No third party product imagery or logos are present.

## Approval

The five deterministic 1920 by 960 marketplace images and their contact sheet are approved for the Peripheral Battery Panel 1.0.0 release candidate.

Any future product UI or Rat Art tooling change requires the relevant capture and visual review gate to rerun.
