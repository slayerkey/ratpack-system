# Rat Validate

Read the product registry, current sales data, prior validations, market research, and the applicable marketplace references.

Research current demand and competitors on the web when freshness matters.

Use the existing deterministic opportunity scorer where applicable, then add the qualitative evidence required by the current RatPack validation rubric.

Record the competitor table, demand evidence, pricing evidence, feature gap, risks, recommended listing name, price, keywords, device or variant plan, score, confidence, and verdict.

Write the validation result even for a rejection so rejected research remains an asset.

Update canonical product state only after the evidence document is complete.

Do not invent product functionality during validation. Treat unsupported behavior as feasibility risk.
